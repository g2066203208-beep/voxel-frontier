## Demiurge procedural planet reference

Voxel Frontier's R16 terrain authority is an independent C++ adaptation of architectural ideas studied from:

- `owenyuwono/demiurge` — https://github.com/owenyuwono/demiurge
- MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 owenyuwono

The referenced project demonstrates a deterministic cube-sphere terrain pipeline combining spherical tectonics, uplift forcing, Priority-Flood depression filling, multiple-flow-direction routing, stream-power erosion/deposition, and thermal talus relaxation. Voxel Frontier reimplements those ideas for its native C++23/Vulkan runtime rather than embedding the TypeScript source.
