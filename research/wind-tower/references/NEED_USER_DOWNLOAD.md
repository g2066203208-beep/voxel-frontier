1. **Li Zeyu, Xu Bin, Wang Jiang, Chen Hongbing, Ge Hanbin.**

   **Engineering Structures 279 (2023), 115622.**  
   DOI: **10.1016/j.engstruct.2023.115622**

   DOI: **10.1016/j.istruc.2024.107235**
