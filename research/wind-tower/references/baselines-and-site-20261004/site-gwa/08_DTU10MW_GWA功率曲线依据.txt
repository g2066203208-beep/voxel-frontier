DTU 10 MW RWT - Global Wind Atlas custom turbine input

Geometry and control parameters
- Rated power: 10,000 kW
- Rotor diameter: 178.3 m
- Hybrid tower total length: 158 m (112 m reinforced-concrete section + 46 m steel section)
- Hub height required by Global Wind Atlas: 160.0 m
- Tower/hub-height source: 大型混塔式风力机的建模与可靠度分析_何泽瑜.pdf, Chapter 3
- Cut-in wind speed: 4 m/s
- Rated wind speed: 11.4 m/s
- Cut-out wind speed: 25 m/s
- Control: variable speed and collective pitch
- Reference air density: 1.225 kg/m3
- IEC Class I design annual mean wind speed: 10 m/s

Power-curve construction
1. Aerodynamic power points at 5, 6, 8, 9, 10 and 11 m/s are taken from the official DTU 10 MW RWT CFD power_curve.dat.
2. Electrical power is calculated using the official 94% electrical generator efficiency from DTU10MWReferenceWindTurbine.xls.
3. Missing below-rated points are linearly interpolated.
4. At 11.4-25 m/s, electrical power is limited to the official 10,000 kW rated constant-power value.
5. This curve is a documented reconstruction from public DTU reference-model data, not a manufacturer-certified measured power curve.

Do not use the Generic 15.0 MW Offshore power curve for the DTU 10 MW model.
