H I G H L I G H T S

• Assess the spatiotemporal variation of the power law exponent (α) in global wind energy assessments.
• The power law exponent α values vary with geography, seasons, diurnal cycles, latitudes, land and ocean.
• The power law is generally applicable and has important implications for wind energy resource assessments and turbine design.
