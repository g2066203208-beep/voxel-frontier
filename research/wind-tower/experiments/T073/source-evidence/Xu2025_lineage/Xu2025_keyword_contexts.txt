Since simulating reinforcement stirrups is challenging in OpenSEES, an approximate modeling approach using the Mander confined concrete model is adopted to simulate the strengthening effect of stirrups and longitudinal reinforcements on concrete. The same fiber approach is applied to steel tower segments. In the nonlinear models, both material and geometric nonlineari- ties are incorporated within OpenSEES.
